I'm a New York City-based actor working in film and television, training in scene study and on-camera work at The Barrow Group.

I grew up in Virginia in an engineering family, but as a child found that acting in the local community theater helped stimulate my artistic impulses. Making something out of nothing is what inspires me most. I channeled that into a decade-long career as a software developer, where I could turn an idea into something real and delightful. As an adult, I grew more passionate about artistic creation as well, and that led me to reconnect with acting.

Off set, I've played soccer since I was four years old. I'm a prolific movie watcher, and I still love to make things, from oil painting to sewing and garment making. I made that blue shirt in my headshot (I drafted the pattern too).
